# Lab 2 - Lean, Six Sigma, Waste, Voice of Customer, and Value

## Objective

Identify customer value, voice of customer, waste, defects, and variation in a simple process.

## Scenario

Employees complain that IT support tickets are slow and status updates are unclear. The team needs to understand what customers value before choosing improvements.

## Tasks

### Step 1 - Capture voice of customer

Create a VOC table:

| Customer statement | Need | CTQ requirement |
|--------------------|------|-----------------|

Example: "I do not know when my ticket will be handled" becomes a need for clear status visibility.

### Step 2 - Identify value-added work

Create a process activity table:

| Activity | Value-added, business-value, or non-value-added | Reason |
|----------|-------------------------------------------------|--------|

### Step 3 - Conduct a waste walk

Find examples of the eight wastes:

- Defects
- Overproduction
- Waiting
- Non-utilized talent
- Transportation
- Inventory
- Motion
- Extra processing

### Step 4 - Connect Six Sigma to defects and variation

Define:

- What is a defect in this process?
- What is an opportunity for defect?
- What variation is visible?
- What data would prove the issue?

## Deliverable

Submit VOC table, value-added table, waste walk table, and defect/variation notes.

## Checkpoint questions

1. What is the difference between waste and a defect?
2. Why should customer needs be translated into CTQs?
3. Which waste appears most often in your scenario?
