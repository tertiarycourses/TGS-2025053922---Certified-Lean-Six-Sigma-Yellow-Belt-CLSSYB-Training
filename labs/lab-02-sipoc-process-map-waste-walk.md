# Lab 02 - SIPOC, Process Map, and Waste Walk

## Objectives

- Build a SIPOC.
- Draw a current-state process map.
- Identify value-added and non-value-added work.
- Locate the eight wastes.

## Scenario

The selected process needs to be understood before it can be improved. You will map the current state and mark waste that affects flow, quality, or customer value.

## Steps

1. Define the process start point and end point.
2. List suppliers.
3. List inputs.
4. List 5 to 10 major process steps.
5. List outputs.
6. List customers.
7. Build a SIPOC table.
8. Draw a process map with steps, decisions, handoffs, queues, and rework loops.
9. Label each step as value-added, business-required, or non-value-added.
10. Review the process for transport, inventory, motion, waiting, overproduction, overprocessing, defects, and underused skills.
11. Mark waste examples on the map.
12. Choose the two most important waste areas for measurement.

## Validation

- SIPOC and process map match the same scope.
- Current-state work is mapped honestly.
- Waste examples are linked to specific process steps.
- Measurement points are identified.

## Deliverables

- SIPOC.
- Current-state process map.
- Waste walk table.
- Measurement point notes.

## Review Questions

1. Why map the current state before proposing improvements?
2. What is the difference between value-added and business-required work?
3. Which waste appears most often in your process?
4. How does mapping help with data collection?
