# Lab 03 - Stakeholders, Facilitation, and Kaizen

## Objectives

- Identify stakeholders.
- Plan a facilitated improvement session.
- Design a small Kaizen activity.
- Start an A3 report.

## Scenario

Improvement depends on people who know the process. You will plan how to involve the right people, structure the conversation, and document the improvement story.

## Steps

1. List people affected by the process issue.
2. Rate each stakeholder by interest and influence.
3. Identify supporters, blockers, and subject matter experts.
4. Write one engagement action for each key stakeholder.
5. Create a 60-minute meeting agenda.
6. Include objective, ground rules, roles, timing, and expected outputs.
7. Choose one scoped issue for a Kaizen activity.
8. Define pre-work, event activities, follow-up actions, and success criteria.
9. Create an A3 report with background, current condition, target condition, and next steps.
10. Present the A3 draft to a peer and collect feedback.

## Validation

- Stakeholder actions match stakeholder concerns.
- Meeting agenda has clear outputs.
- Kaizen scope is small and practical.
- A3 draft explains the project clearly.

## Deliverables

- Stakeholder analysis.
- Facilitation agenda.
- Kaizen plan.
- Draft A3 report.

## Review Questions

1. Why is facilitation important in process improvement?
2. What makes a Kaizen event focused?
3. Why should stakeholders be engaged early?
4. How does A3 reporting support communication?
