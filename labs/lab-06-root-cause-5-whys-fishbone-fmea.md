# Lab 06 - Root Cause, 5 Whys, Fishbone, and FMEA

## Objectives

- Use 5 Whys.
- Build a Fishbone diagram.
- Identify evidence needed for causes.
- Create a basic FMEA.

## Scenario

The team has a focus issue. You will explore possible causes and prioritize process risks before choosing countermeasures.

## Steps

1. Select the main issue from Lab 04 or Lab 05.
2. Write a fact-based problem statement.
3. Complete 5 Whys for the issue.
4. Stop when the cause becomes process-related rather than blame-based.
5. Create a Fishbone diagram.
6. Use categories such as people, process, tools, materials, measurement, and environment.
7. Add possible causes under each category.
8. Circle causes that need evidence.
9. Create an FMEA for the process step most related to the problem.
10. List failure mode, effect, cause, and current controls.
11. Score severity, occurrence, and detection.
12. Calculate RPN.
13. Identify the top two risks for action.

## Validation

- Problem statement is factual.
- 5 Whys does not end with blame.
- Fishbone causes are plausible.
- FMEA identifies risks and current controls.

## Deliverables

- Problem statement.
- 5 Whys worksheet.
- Fishbone diagram.
- Evidence-needed list.
- FMEA.
- Prioritized risk list.

## Review Questions

1. Why should causes be checked with evidence?
2. What makes a good 5 Whys chain?
3. What is the purpose of FMEA?
4. How does RPN help prioritize action?
