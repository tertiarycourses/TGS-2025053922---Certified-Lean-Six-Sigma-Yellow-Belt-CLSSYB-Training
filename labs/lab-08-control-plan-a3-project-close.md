# Lab 08 - Control Plan, A3, and Project Close

## Objectives

- Create a control plan.
- Define response actions.
- Finalize an A3 report.
- Present the improvement story.

## Scenario

The improvement idea has been selected or tested. You must make sure the process owner knows what to monitor, what to do when performance changes, and how the improvement story is documented.

## Steps

1. List improved process steps that require control.
2. Identify the KPI or CTQ to monitor.
3. Define target, measurement method, frequency, and owner.
4. Write a response plan for performance outside the expected range.
5. Finalize standard work instructions.
6. Define training or handover actions.
7. Compare before and after performance if pilot data is available.
8. Update the A3 report with problem, current state, analysis, countermeasure, result, and follow-up.
9. Prepare a five-minute presentation.
10. Present to a peer or trainer.
11. Record feedback, lessons learned, and remaining risks.

## Validation

- Control plan has clear owner and frequency.
- Response plan states what to do when performance changes.
- A3 report tells the complete improvement story.
- Handover actions are practical.

## Deliverables

- Control plan.
- Response plan.
- Final standard work.
- Final A3 report.
- Presentation notes.
- Lessons learned.

## Review Questions

1. Why do improvements fade without control?
2. What should a response plan include?
3. How does A3 reporting support project closure?
4. What should be handed over to the process owner?
