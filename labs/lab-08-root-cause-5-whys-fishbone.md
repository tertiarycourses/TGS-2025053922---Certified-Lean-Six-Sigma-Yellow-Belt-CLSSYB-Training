# Lab 8 - Root Cause Analysis with 5 Whys, Fishbone, and Evidence

## Objective

Support root cause analysis using 5 Whys, fishbone diagram categories, and evidence checks.

## Scenario

Pareto analysis shows delayed assignment is the largest defect category. The team asks you to help identify possible causes.

## Tasks

### Step 1 - Write the problem

Use one focused problem:

```text
Ticket assignment is delayed for <percentage> of tickets during <period>, causing <impact>.
```

### Step 2 - Complete 5 Whys

Create:

| Why level | Answer | Evidence needed |
|-----------|--------|-----------------|

Stop when you reach a cause that can be tested or controlled.

### Step 3 - Create fishbone diagram

Use categories:

- People
- Process
- Policy
- Technology
- Measurement
- Environment

Add possible causes under each.

### Step 4 - Prioritize likely causes

Create:

| Possible cause | Evidence available | Confidence high/medium/low |
|----------------|--------------------|-----------------------------|

### Step 5 - Validate before solution

Write what data, observation, interview, or test would confirm each likely cause.

## Deliverable

Submit problem statement, 5 Whys table, fishbone diagram, cause priority table, and validation plan.

## Checkpoint questions

1. Why should root cause analysis use evidence?
2. What makes a cause actionable?
3. Why should the team avoid jumping straight to solutions?
