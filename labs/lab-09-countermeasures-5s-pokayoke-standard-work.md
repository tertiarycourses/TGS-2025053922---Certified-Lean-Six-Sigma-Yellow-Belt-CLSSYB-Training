# Lab 9 - Countermeasures, 5S, Mistake Proofing, Standard Work, and Kaizen

## Objective

Select practical countermeasures using 5S, mistake proofing, visual management, standard work, and Kaizen planning.

## Scenario

Root cause analysis suggests delayed assignments happen because ticket categories are inconsistent and required information is missing. The team wants low-risk countermeasures.
